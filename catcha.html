Privacy Policy

Updated: November 5, 2026

Support

Catcha is an iPhone app that stores notification information received from Apple Shortcuts on your device, so you can review it by date. No app-specific account is required.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the source app name, title, body, receipt time and app icon passed by your chosen shortcut, bookmark status, and preferences such as language, colors and retention period. Notification content may contain personal information.

Developer collection and transmission

Catcha does not send notification history to the developer's servers. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not provide its own cloud sync.

Shortcuts and widgets

You configure the source apps and automation in Apple Shortcuts. Notification information and icons needed by widgets are shared locally between the app and widget extension. Content may be visible on your Home Screen or Lock Screen; adjust iOS display settings or widget placement as needed.

Retention, deletion and backups

You can delete individual notifications and change the automatic deletion period. Bookmarked notifications do not auto-delete. Device backups may include app data. Shortcut sync, backups and data handling when removing the app also depend on Apple's services and your settings.

External pages and services

The privacy and support page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, the hosting provider may process connection information such as your IP address. This page adds no analytics or advertising. Apple's and GitHub's own privacy policies apply to their services.

Contact and updates

If you send an email, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond to your request. The app does not automatically attach notification content, history or logs. Avoid unnecessary personal information. Policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Support

Describe a problem or suggest a feature. Include your app and iOS versions if useful. Email is sent only when you choose to send it.

If notifications are not being recorded, check the selected apps and Automation in Shortcuts, then try a new notification. Past notifications cannot be imported.
